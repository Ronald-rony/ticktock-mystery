Privacy Policy for TickTock Mystery

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collected

TickTock Mystery is designed specifically for children and families. We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal or device information whatsoever.

  • No names, emails, photos, locations, or Advertising IDs are collected.
  • No third-party advertisements or ad SDKs are included.
  • No analytics, behavioral tracking, or crash telemetry SDKs are included.
  • No in-app purchases, external links, or social chat features exist.

2. Local Offline Storage Only

Game progress (stars, streaks, unlocked badges, selected clock skins, and audio settings) is stored strictly on the user's device using local storage. This data never leaves the device and can be reset anytime via the in-app Parents Gate or by uninstalling the app.

3. Children's Privacy Compliance

TickTock Mystery is built to comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), Google Play Families Policy, Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines, and GDPR-K.

4. Contact

If parents or guardians have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out using the developer contact email listed on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store page.
