# Privacy Policy for TickTock Mystery

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026  
**Developer:** TickTock Mystery Studio  

## 1. Zero Data Collection (100% Kid-Safe)
**TickTock Mystery** is built specifically for children and families. We **do not collect, store, share, or sell** any personal information or usage data whatsoever.

Specifically:
- **No personal information** (names, emails, photos, locations, or device identifiers) is collected.
- **No third-party advertising** or ad SDKs exist anywhere in the app.
- **No analytics, telemetry, or crash-tracking SDKs** are included.
- **No in-app purchases** or external purchase links exist.
- **No social media links, chat, or external web navigation** accessible to children.

## 2. Offline Local Storage Only
All game progress (stars, streaks, unlocked badges, selected clock skins, and audio preferences) is stored **exclusively on the user's device** using local app storage. This data never leaves the device and can be cleared anytime through the **Grown-Ups Only (Parents Gate)** menu or by uninstalling the app.

## 3. Compliance
TickTock Mystery is designed to comply with:
- **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- **Apple App Store Kids Category** guidelines (including an in-app Parents Gate)
- **Google Play Families Policy**
- **GDPR-K** (Children's data protection under GDPR)

## 4. Contact
If parents or guardians have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the App Store / Google Play Store listing page.
